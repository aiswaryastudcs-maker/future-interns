# Prompts Used

## Business
I selected a local salon for this task. The business name is Glow & Grace Salon.

## Homepage Prompt

Write simple and attractive homepage content for Glow & Grace Salon. Include a headline, short introduction, what makes the salon special, and a clear call to action. Keep the content suitable for a local salon website.

## Services Prompt

Write short descriptions for the following services:
- Haircut & Styling
- Hair Spa
- Facial
- Bridal Makeup

The descriptions should be easy to understand and useful for customers.

## CTA Prompt

Write a few simple call-to-action lines for Glow & Grace Salon. Encourage customers to book an appointment or contact the salon.

## Tone Prompt

Rewrite the content in a friendly, stylish and professional tone. Keep it simple and avoid unnecessary words.
